---
name: Customer price-list privacy
description: Keep shareable Sandulon price lists separate from internal supply-chain pricing.
---

Customer-facing price lists may include published product prices and order terms, but must not include production costs or supplier details. Build the shareable list from the site's published catalogue and customer terms, not the internal master pricing document.

**Why:** Customers may save and forward the list; the internal source includes confidential production and supply-chain information.

**How to apply:** When updating published prices or terms, keep the shareable list aligned with the customer-facing site and inspect the generated print output for cost or supplier disclosures.
